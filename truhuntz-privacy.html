Privacy Policy

Effective as of October 9, 2026

⚠️ Draft notice: this policy has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. It accurately describes what the TruHuntz app currently collects and does, but should not be relied on as a substitute for professional legal or regulatory advice.

This privacy policy applies to the TruHuntz app for iPhone, including its home screen and lock screen widgets, together with any related services operated by Robert Long (collectively, the “Application”). Robert Long is hereby referred to as the “Service Provider”.

The short version

  • You can use the map, pins, field logger and weather without an account. Without an account, your pins and logs stay on your phone.
  • An optional free account unlocks offline map downloads and backing up your pins and logs. Backed-up pins and logs are stored in your account and are private to you.
  • We do not show ads, use advertising or cross-app tracking, or sell your information.
  • You can delete your account, and your backed-up data, inside the app.

One account for TruHuntz and TruGuidz

TruHuntz and TruGuidz (our outdoor-guide booking app) use the same sign-in. If you create an account in TruHuntz, a matching TruGuidz profile is created for you automatically, containing the name and email address you signed up with. If you already have a TruGuidz account, you sign in to TruHuntz with it. Deleting your account in either app closes it in both, as described under “Deleting your account” below. TruGuidz has its own privacy policy for what it collects.

Information Collection and Use

Used on your phone only (no account needed):

  • Your location. With your permission, while the app is open, the Application reads your precise location to show you on the map, to place pins, to tag field logs with where they happened, and to look up local weather. The Application does not track your location in the background.
  • Pins and field logs. Pins you drop (stands, trail cameras, rubs, scrapes, bedding, entry routes) and field logs you record (sightings, shots, harvests, sign), including their time, location, weather conditions, and any species, weapon, and notes you enter.
  • Offline maps. Map areas you download are stored on your phone.
  • Widget settings. The stand you choose for the widget (its name and coordinates) and the latest wind and weather are shared between the app and its widget on your phone.

If you create an account, we also collect:

  • Your name, email address, and password (stored by our authentication provider in hashed form — we cannot read it).
  • A username generated for you and an optional display name.
  • A backup copy of your pins and field logs (the same details described above).
  • Standard server and hosting logs collected automatically by our infrastructure providers, which may include your IP address and device type.

The Application does not use our own analytics tools to track which screens you visit.

Location, Weather and Maps

  • Apple WeatherKit. To show weather and wind, the Application sends coordinates (your location, or the location of a stand you choose) to Apple’s WeatherKit service, and the widget does the same when it refreshes. Apple’s handling of that data is described in Apple’s WeatherKit information and its privacy policy.
  • Mapbox. Maps are provided by Mapbox. When you view or download a map, the Application requests map data for the area shown, so Mapbox receives your IP address and the map area requested. The Mapbox software in the Application may also send anonymous usage and location-related telemetry to Mapbox for app functionality and analytics. This is not linked to your name or account. You can opt out in the app: tap the “i” button at the bottom of the map and choose “Mapbox Telemetry”. See Mapbox’s privacy policy.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

TruHuntz does not use advertising SDKs, cookies, pixels, or cross-app tracking, and does not use your data to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If this changes in the future, the Service Provider will update this policy and, where required by applicable law, obtain consent first.

Information Visible to Other Users

Your pins and field logs are private to you. They are never shown to other users, and the Application has no feature for sharing them. Your name, email address, and password are not shown to other users either.

Future features, such as a social feed, will be described here before they are released, and any posts you make would be visible to other users as part of how those features work.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by the Service Provider. To exercise these rights, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, contact the Service Provider at longrobert346@yahoo.com.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account). This immediately deletes the backed-up pins, field logs, and TruHuntz profile stored in your account, and closes your Tru account, which disables sign-in to both TruHuntz and TruGuidz and removes the personal details on your TruGuidz profile. Records TruGuidz must keep for other people (for example, a past booking involving another user) are retained in anonymized form. Data stored only on your phone, including offline maps, stays there until you delete the app.
