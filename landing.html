How it works

Built for both sides of the trip.

Whether you're looking to book your next trip or share your own expertise as a guide, TruGuidz handles the logistics so you don't have to.

For Explorers

  • Browse real listings from local guides — photos, pricing, availability
  • Filter by region, trip type, and guide rating
  • Request a booking for the exact dates and group size you need
  • Message your guide directly once a trip is confirmed
  • Pay securely in the app — your card is only charged once the guide accepts

For Guides

  • Create listings for your trips in minutes
  • Set your own pricing, group size, and availability
  • Manage incoming requests and your calendar in one place
  • Get paid directly through secure Stripe payouts
  • Build your reputation with reviews from past trips
Trust & safety
