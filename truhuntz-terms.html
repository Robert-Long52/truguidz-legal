These terms apply to the TruHuntz feed, where signed-in users can share hunts with other hunters. They are in addition to the TruHuntz Privacy Policy. Robert Long is the “Service Provider”. By accepting these terms in the app, you agree to them.

What you may post

You may post photos and text about your own hunts and outdoor experiences. You are responsible for everything you post, and you confirm that you own it or have the right to share it, and that it shows lawful activity.

What you may not post

  • Hunts or harvests that break the law, including out-of-season, over-limit, trespass, spotlighting or other poaching, and anything that could reasonably be mistaken for it.
  • Exact locations of any kind: coordinates, map screenshots, GPS overlays, or text that identifies a specific private property, stand, camera or bedding area. Share a county or town only.
  • Content that is gratuitously graphic, such as mutilation or gore beyond what is ordinarily shown in a harvest photo.
  • Nudity or sexual content, harassment, threats, hate speech, or content that targets a person or group.
  • Spam, advertising, scams, impersonation, or content that infringes someone else’s rights or privacy (including photos of other people without their consent).
  • Anything involving minors that is not appropriate for a general audience.

Reporting and blocking

Every post has a menu where you can report it or block its author. Blocking hides each person’s posts from the other. You can review and undo blocks in Account → Blocked users. Posts reported by several different people are hidden automatically until they have been reviewed.

How we enforce these terms

We review reports and aim to act on them within 24 hours. We may remove any post, restrict features, or suspend or close any account that breaks these terms, with or without notice. Serious or unlawful content may be reported to the authorities. You can appeal a decision by emailing longrobert346@yahoo.com.

Your content and our rights

You keep ownership of what you post. By posting, you give the Service Provider a non-exclusive, worldwide licence to host and display it in the TruHuntz feed for as long as it is posted. Deleting a post removes it from the feed and removes its photos. Deleting your account deletes your posts and photos.

Posts are public to other users

Posts in the feed are visible to other signed-in TruHuntz users, including your display name, username, photos, caption, the county or town you entered, and any weather you chose to include. Your pins, field logs, email address and exact locations are never shown in the feed. Photos are re-encoded in the app before upload, which removes the location and camera details embedded in them.

No guarantees

The feed is provided as is. We do not verify what users post (including that a hunt was lawful) and are not responsible for user content. Wind, weather and scent information in the app is an estimate and is not a substitute for your own judgment and for the laws of where you hunt.

Changes

We may update these terms. If we make material changes, the app will ask you to accept the new version before you can continue to use the feed.
