TruHuntz

Flagship iOS app

Know where your scent is going before you climb the stand. Pick any pin and TruHuntz draws a scent cone from that exact spot using the wind forecast for that location.

  • Wind and scent cone from any pin, so you can plan a sit the night before
  • Home-screen and lock-screen widgets with the next eight hours of wind
  • Private pins for stands, trail cameras, rubs, scrapes and bedding
  • Topographic and satellite maps, with a dark interface for low light
  • Free to use, with an optional account that backs up your pins
  • SwiftUI
  • WidgetKit
  • Supabase
  • iOS 17+

TruKit

Developer starter kit

A SwiftUI + Supabase boilerplate for iOS. Skip the setup chores and start on the app only you can build.

  • Email and Sign in with Apple auth, with in-app account deletion built for App Review
  • Paywall with Stripe, StoreKit 2 or RevenueCat behind one protocol
  • Real-time map, Row Level Security and four Edge Functions, all documented
  • Demo mode that runs the whole app before you connect a backend
From $49 Standard, one app · $149 Agency, unlimited apps

TruGuidz

Guide marketplace

Connects explorers with real, local guides for hunting, fishing, hiking and trail-riding trips across America. Browse, book and pay in one place.

  • Verified guides, real reviews and secure Stripe payments
  • Request exact dates and group size, then message your guide
  • Guides can sign up in the app or on the web portal
